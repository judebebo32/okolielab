Personalised tutorials and practice cubes

Stuck on a step, or teaching a group and want help? Dr. Okolie offers personalised tutorials on the 2×2, 3×3, Pyraminx, mirror cube, 4×4, Megaminx and Helicopter cube. If you don't have a puzzle yet, ask for one to practise with.

  • Students and beginners
    One-on-one help to get your first solve.
  • Classes and clubs
    Using the cube to explain systems thinking and problem solving.
  • Practice cubes
    Ask, and we'll be in touch about sending one.
