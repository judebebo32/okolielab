How to solve the Helicopter cube

It looks like a cube, but it turns around its edges, and an uneven turn sends it into wild non-cube shapes. Corners first, then centres.

Notation

Reading the moves

The Helicopter cube turns around its edges, not its faces. Each move is a half turn around one edge, named after the two faces that meet there. Because every move is a half turn, there is no clockwise or counter-clockwise, and doing the same move twice undoes it.

Only make full half turns. If you stop part-way, the pieces can jump into a strange non-cube shape. That's called jumbling, and it's fun later but confusing now. If it happens, turn back.

UFHalf turn (180°) around the edge between the top (U) and front (F) faces
UR, UL, UBThe other three top edges
FR, FLThe two front vertical edges
DF, DR…Edges on the bottom work the same way
Step 1

Put the corners in place

Solve the corners first and ignore the triangle-shaped centres for now. Every turn swaps the two corners at the ends of that edge (and twists them).

Pick one corner as your reference; every other corner then has one correct place in the colour scheme (white opposite yellow, green opposite blue, red opposite orange). Move the corners home one at a time: turning the edge between two corners swaps them. To swap two corners that don't share an edge, use three swaps through a corner between them: A↔B, then B↔C, then A↔B again. That swaps A and C and leaves B where it was. Don't worry about twisted corners yet.

Step 2

Twist the corners

Hold the cube so a twisted corner is at the top front-right, with a neighbour at the top front-left. There are two algorithms, and each twists both front corners at once:

UR UL UB UL UR UF

moves the top sticker of both front corners down onto the front face, and

UF UR UL UB UL UR

moves the top sticker of each front corner out to the side (right for the right corner, left for the left). Use one of them to fix the front-right corner. The front-left corner gets twisted too, so make it your next corner: turn the whole cube so it becomes the front-right corner and repeat with its next neighbour. Work your way around the top, then the bottom. When you reach the last corner, it will already be right.

Step 3

Solve the centres

The centres are solved with one algorithm that swaps three centres in a loop without touching the corners:

FR UF FR UR FR UF FR UR

It moves the centre at spot 1 (front face, upper-left) to spot 2 (top face, front-right), spot 2 to spot 3 (right face, upper-back), and spot 3 back to spot 1. Doing the moves in reverse order sends them the other way.

To use it on any centre: first do one or two setup moves that bring the centre you want into one of the three spots, without moving the other two spots. Do the algorithm, then undo your setup moves in reverse order. Because the corners are only moved by the setup moves, undoing them puts the corners back. Work one face at a time. Centres of the same colour are identical, so any matching centre will do.

Step 4

Done

Solved! Once you're comfortable, try jumbling turns. They take the puzzle into shapes no cube can make.
