How to solve the triangle (Pyraminx)

The pyramid puzzle looks harder than a cube but is easier: four steps and two algorithms for the finish.

Notation

Reading the moves

Hold the Pyraminx with one face toward you and one corner pointing up. Its four corners are U (top), L (bottom-left), R (bottom-right) and B (back).

U, L, R, BTurn the two layers around the top, left, right or back corner clockwise, as if you were looking at that corner from outside
u, l, r, bTurn only the small tip at that corner
U'Counter-clockwise
Step 1

Turn the tips

The four small tips at the corners turn on their own and never get in the way. Turn each tip until it matches the three centre triangles just below it. Done.

Step 2

Line up the centres

Choose a colour for the bottom. One corner has three centres that don't include that colour; point that corner up. Now turn each of the three bottom corners (L, R, B) until its centre on the bottom face shows your bottom colour. Finally turn U so the top centres match the ones below them.

Step 3

Solve the bottom edges

Work on the edge at the front of the bottom face. Find the edge piece with the bottom colour and the front colour. If it is in the top layer, find its case below and do that algorithm (each one restores the top centres at the end). Then turn the whole puzzle, keeping the bottom down, and repeat for the other two bottom edges.

Edge is at the…Bottom colour faces the…Do
front-right top edgeright faceL' U L U'
front-right top edgefront faceU R U' R'
front-left top edgeleft faceR U' R' U
front-left top edgefront faceL R' L' R
back top edgeright faceR U R' U'
back top edgeleft faceL' U' L U

If the edge is already in the bottom but in the wrong place or flipped, do any of these algorithms once to bring it up to the top, then insert it.

Step 4

Solve the last three edges

Only the three edges around the top corner are left. First, get each edge into the right position even if it is flipped. If they need to cycle, do:

R U R' U R U R'

If that sent them the wrong way round, do it once more. Then, if two edges are flipped, turn the whole puzzle (bottom still down) so the flipped two are at the front-left and front-right, and do:

R' L R L' U L' U' L

Step 5

Done

Solved. The Pyraminx has far fewer positions than a 3×3, so with a little practice you'll be solving it in under a minute.
