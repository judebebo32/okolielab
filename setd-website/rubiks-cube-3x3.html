How to solve a 3×3 Rubik's cube

The layer-by-layer beginner method: seven steps and six short algorithms. Colours below use the standard scheme (white opposite yellow, green opposite blue, red opposite orange).

Notation

Reading the moves

Each letter is one turn of one face. "Clockwise" always means clockwise as if you were looking straight at that face. Hold the cube the way each step says, and keep it in that position while you do a sequence.

RRight face clockwise
LLeft face clockwise
UUp (top) face clockwise
DDown (bottom) face clockwise
FFront face clockwise
BBack face clockwise
R'A prime (') means counter-clockwise
R2A 2 means a half turn (180°)
Step 1

Make the white cross

Hold the cube with the yellow centre on top. Bring the four white edge pieces up around it to make a "daisy": a yellow centre with four white petals. Edges can be moved up without any algorithm; just turn the face that holds one until it reaches the top, then turn the top so you don't knock off a petal you already placed.

Now turn the top until one petal's side colour matches the centre below it, then hold that petal at the front and turn the front face twice (F2). The white edge drops to the bottom. Repeat for all four petals. Flip the cube over and you should see a white cross whose side colours match each side centre.

Step 2

Solve the white corners

Hold the cube with white on the bottom from now on. Find a corner in the top layer that has white on it. Turn the top layer until that corner sits directly above the place it belongs (between the two centres that match its other two colours), then turn the whole cube so that place is at the front-right.

Repeat R U R' U' until the corner drops into place with white facing down. It takes one to five repeats. If a white corner is stuck in the bottom layer in the wrong spot, hold it at the front-right and do R U R' U' once to bring it up.

Step 3

Solve the middle layer

Find an edge in the top layer with no yellow on it. Turn the top until the colour on its front matches the front centre, making an upside-down T. Look at its top colour. If it matches the right-hand centre, the edge goes to the right:

U R U' R' U' F' U F

If it matches the left-hand centre, the edge goes to the left:

U' L' U L U F U' F'

If a middle edge is in the wrong slot or flipped, use either algorithm to push any top edge into that slot. That brings the wrong edge back up so you can insert it properly.

Step 4

Make the yellow cross

Look only at the yellow stickers on top, ignoring corners. You will see a dot, an L, a line, or a cross already. The algorithm is F R U R' U' F'.

  • Dot: do the algorithm once to get an L.
  • L: hold it so the two yellow edges point to the back and left (like a clock reading 9:00 to 12:00), then do the algorithm to get a line.
  • Line: hold it horizontally (left to right), then do the algorithm to get the cross.
Step 5

Match the yellow edges

Turn the top until at least two yellow edges have side colours that match their centres. If the two matching edges are next to each other, hold them at the back and the right, then do:

R U R' U R U2 R' U

If the two matching edges are opposite each other, do the algorithm once from any side, then turn the top to find two adjacent matching edges and do it again.

Step 6

Put the yellow corners in place

A corner is "in place" when it sits between the three centres that match its colours, even if it is twisted. Find a corner that is already in place and hold it at the front-right of the top layer. Then do:

U R U' L' U R' U' L

Repeat until all four corners are in place. If no corner is in place yet, do the algorithm once from any side and look again.

Step 7

Twist the yellow corners

Hold the cube with yellow on top and an unsolved corner at the front-right. Repeat R' D' R D until that corner shows yellow on top (two or four times). The bottom layers will look scrambled. That's expected, so keep going.

Now turn only the top layer (U) to bring the next unsolved corner to the front-right, and repeat. Don't turn the whole cube. When every corner shows yellow on top, the rest of the cube comes back together. Turn the top layer to finish.
