Rubik's cube tutorials

How to solve a 2×2 cube

The 2×2 (pocket cube) is only corners, so it needs just three algorithms. It's the quickest way to learn how a cube moves.

Notation

Reading the moves

Each letter is one turn of one face. "Clockwise" always means clockwise as if you were looking straight at that face. Hold the cube the way each step says, and keep it in that position while you do a sequence.

RRight face clockwise
LLeft face clockwise
UUp (top) face clockwise
DDown (bottom) face clockwise
FFront face clockwise
BBack face clockwise
R'A prime (') means counter-clockwise
R2A 2 means a half turn (180°)
Step 1

Solve one layer

A 2×2 has no centres, so pick a colour (white is a good start) and build that face, making sure the side colours of the four corners match each other in pairs.

It's the same as the 3×3 corner step. Hold white on the bottom, put a white corner in the top layer directly above where it belongs at the front-right, and repeat R U R' U' until it drops in with white facing down.

Step 2

Make the top face yellow

Hold yellow on top. Put a corner that does not show yellow on top at the front-right, and repeat R' D' R D until yellow faces up (two or four times). The bottom layer will look broken for a while. Don't worry.

Turn only the top layer (U) to bring the next corner without yellow on top to the front-right, and repeat. Once the whole top is yellow, the bottom layer is restored.

Step 3

Swap the top corners into place

Look at the sides of the top layer for headlights: two corners on the same side whose side colours match. Hold the headlights at the back and do:

R' F R' B2 R F' R' B2 R2

If there are no headlights on any side, do the algorithm once from any side; headlights will appear. Then turn the top layer to line it up with the bottom.

Step 4

Done

That's the whole 2×2. Because it is just the corners of a 3×3, everything you learned here carries straight over to the 3×3 tutorial.
