How to solve the Megaminx

The 12-sided Megaminx is solved just like a 3×3, one layer at a time, only with more layers. If you can solve a 3×3, every idea here will feel familiar.

Notation

Reading the moves

Hold the Megaminx with the white face at the bottom for the whole tutorial. One of the five upper faces points at you: that is F. "Clockwise" means clockwise as if you were looking straight at that face. To work on a different spot, turn the whole puzzle, keeping white at the bottom.

UTop face, one-fifth turn (72°) clockwise
FFront face: the upper face pointing at you
R, LThe upper faces to the right and left of F
BR, BLThe two upper faces at the back
DR, DLThe lower faces at the front-right and front-left (below where F meets R or L)
DThe bottom face (white)
R'A prime (') means counter-clockwise
U2, U2'Two-fifths of a turn (144°) clockwise or counter-clockwise
Step 1

Make the white star

Bring the five white edges down next to the white centre, each with its other colour matching the centre of the face beside it. This is intuitive: a lower face turn (DR, DL and so on) swings an edge down into the white face, and top-layer turns let you line it up first. Tip: turn the puzzle upside down while you do this, so you can see the white face.

Step 2

Solve the white corners

Turn the puzzle (white still at the bottom) so the corner slot you want to fill is at the front, between DL, DR and white. Using only U and F turns (they never touch the white face), bring the corner to the spot directly above its slot, where F, DL and DR meet. Then repeat:

DR F DR' F'

until the corner drops in with white facing down (one to five times). If a white corner is in the wrong slot, hold that slot at the front and do the algorithm once to lift it out.

Step 3

Second layer edges

These five edges sit between neighbouring lower faces. Hold the slot you want at the front, between DL and DR. Bring the edge into the front face F (with U and F turns), then turn F until its side colour matches the centre beside it.

If it's on the lower-left of F, matching DL, it goes to the right:

F DR F' DR' F' DL' F DL

If it's on the lower-right of F, matching DR, it goes to the left:

F' DL' F DL F DR F' DR'

These are the 3×3 middle-layer algorithms, with F playing the part of the top face. If an edge is in the wrong slot, use either one to push another edge in, which brings the wrong one back out.

Step 4

Finish the lower faces

Each of the five lower faces still needs one corner and two edges. Work on one front face at a time: its slot is the corner where F, DL and DR meet, plus the edges F–DL and F–DR.

Corner first. Turn U until the corner is at the top-front-left, then do F' to bring it to the front-left middle spot (where F, L and DL meet). Now repeat:

L F L' F'

until the corner is solved (one to five times). Then the two edges: turn U to bring the edge to the top-front, and find its case:

The edge belongs at the…WhenDo
Front and lower-left (F + DL colours)The F colour is on topF L' F' U F L F' U'
Front and lower-left (F + DL colours)The F colour faces the frontF2 U' F2' U' F2 U F2' U
Front and lower-right (F + DR colours)The F colour is on topF' R F U' F' R' F U
Front and lower-right (F + DR colours)The F colour faces the frontF2' U F2 U F2' U' F2 U'

These moves never disturb the other lower faces. If a piece is stuck in the middle ring of the puzzle, turn the puzzle so it's at the front-left middle spot and do F U F' to lift it to the top without breaking anything you've solved. If a piece is in the wrong slot, hold that slot at the front and do the matching algorithm once to swap it out.

Step 5

Fill the upper ring

Now only the top face and the ring of slots just below it remain, and the top face is free to move, just like the last layers of a 3×3. For each slot (the corner where F, R and DR meet, and the edge between F and R):

Corner: turn U to bring it to the top-front-right, then repeat R U R' U' until it drops in correctly.

Edge: turn U until the edge's front colour matches F. If its top colour matches R, do U R U' R' U' F' U F; if it matches L, do U' L' U L U F U' F'.

Step 6

Make the top star

Look at the five top edges. Some show the top colour on top, some don't (there is always an even number that don't). The algorithm is F R U R' U' F'.

  • Two wrong, not next to each other: one at the front, the other at the back-right. Do the algorithm once.
  • Two wrong, next to each other: hold them at the front and front-right. Do it once to get the case above.
  • Four wrong: hold the correct one at the front-right. Do it once to get two wrong next to each other.
Step 7

Match the top edges

Turn U until as many top edges as possible match their side centres. The algorithm is R U R' U R U2' R'; it cycles the three edges at the right and back, leaving the front and left edges in place.

  • Two edges next to each other match: hold them at the front and left. Do the algorithm, then once more if needed.
  • Two edges match but not next to each other: hold one at the front, the other at the back-right. Do the algorithm.
  • Only one matches: hold it at the left. Do the algorithm.

After each one, turn U again to line up as many as possible, and repeat.

Step 8

Put the top corners in place

Don't turn the top any more: the edges are solved. A corner is in place when it sits between the three centres that match its colours, even if twisted. The algorithm is U R U' BL' U R' U' BL.

  • Two neighbouring corners in place: hold them at the top front-left and front-right. Do the algorithm, then once more if needed.
  • Two corners in place, not neighbours: hold one at the front-right, the other at the back (between BR and BL). Do the algorithm.
  • One corner in place: hold it at the front-left. Do the algorithm.
  • None: do it from any side.

Look again after each one and repeat until all five are in place.

Step 9

Twist the top corners

Exactly like the last step of the 3×3. Hold an unsolved corner at the top-front-right and repeat R' DR' R DR until the top colour faces up (two or four times). The rest of the puzzle will look scrambled. Keep going. Turn only U to bring the next unsolved corner to the front-right and repeat. When all five are done, everything comes back; turn the top to line it up.
